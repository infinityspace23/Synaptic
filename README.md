# __*Synaptic*__
## A collation of all codework related to the visualization tool, Synaptic
---
It was after having ran past 8 weeks of CS50x that I wanted to explore other domains of Computer Science. Machine learning albeit a complex and out-of-reach domain, I waddled my way through what I could understand with the help of the Internet and LLMs(ironically). The process of learning about ANNs made me think deeper about knowledge and how much more effort is used to store it, than really talked about since we as humans do it naturally. This codebase is a collation of the code that either I have written (at times with the help of an AI tutor, at no point was anything taken wholesale at-face value) or learnt of from kind strangers on the Internet.
